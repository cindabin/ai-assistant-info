Jarvis AI Assistant

What this app does

Jarvis AI Assistant is a personal Gmail automation tool. It periodically checks the developer’s own inbox and:

  • Labels each incoming message as needing a reply, purely informational, or staged for a summary digest
  • Sends a periodic digest email summarizing everything staged that way

Who uses it

Jarvis AI Assistant has exactly one user: the developer who built it, for their own personal Gmail account. It is not a public product, is not distributed to other users, and does not process data belonging to anyone else.
