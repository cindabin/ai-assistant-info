AI Assistant — Gmail Triage

This is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single developer for their own Gmail inbox. It periodically reviews incoming mail and sorts it into a small set of labels (needs a reply, informational, or staged for a daily summary digest), and can send that summary digest by email.

It is not a public product or service — it has exactly one user, its developer, and is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

See the privacy policy for details on what data it accesses and how.
